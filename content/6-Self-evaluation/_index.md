During my internship at **Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited** from **June 1, 2026** to **July 30, 2026**, I had the opportunity to learn, practice, and apply the knowledge acquired in school to a real-world working environment.
I independently owned and built a **semantic search feature** for `VideoPlatformServer`, a team video-sharing/livestream platform — designing and implementing the `search_service` microservice (Python/FastAPI) end to end, including audio transcription (Whisper), visual captioning fallback (BLIP), dense/sparse embeddings (`multilingual-e5-large`, BM25), vector search (Qdrant, hybrid RRF fusion), retrieval benchmarking, and deployment to AWS SageMaker Serverless Inference. Through this, I improved my skills in **applied machine learning, backend/microservice development, AWS cloud deployment, technical benchmarking, and cross-team collaboration**.

In terms of work ethic, I always strived to complete tasks well, complied with workplace regulations, and actively engaged with colleagues to improve work efficiency.
